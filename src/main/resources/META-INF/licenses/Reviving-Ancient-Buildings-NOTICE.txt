Imported structure: chengbao3.nbt. Seven complete 17x9x17 furnished rooms are
loaded by vanilla LOAD structure blocks inside the original deadrock maze.
The native two-floor recursive maze layout is retained; the whole saved floor
is not copied. The former custom grid planner is no longer used. Old port.9
piece readers and their source template remain solely for saved-piece support.
The original Finale forge is retained below those two levels. zhongyan3.nbt
(End Cube hall) is not imported or substituted for the second basement.
The original mod is not required. Its End Cube entity, combat, client effects,
dimension-height alterations and other structures are not redistributed here.

assets and code, and does not assert permission for public redistribution.
